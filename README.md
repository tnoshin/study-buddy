# Study Buddy

**What is this?**
- A plugin with ready-made instructions that lets Claude help you study more efficiently.

**What does it do?**
- Helps students plan their study routine, study with Claude, and stay accountable at the end of each day.

**Features**
- Study plans and daily routines
- Quizzes and flashcards
- Cheat sheets made from your own notes
- A daily log to check in each evening
- Optional: connect Notion or Google Drive for more personalized help

**How to use it**
- Type a slash (`/`) and it will show you the skills, or
- Just type **"how to use this"** to get started.

Happy learning!

## Who it's for

Anyone preparing for an exam, keeping up with a course, or teaching themselves something. Any subject works, math included.

## Get started

To install from a file: in Claude, go to **Customize → Plugins → Add → Upload plugin** and choose the file.

After installing, start a new chat and ask **"How do I use this plugin?"**, or pick **how-to-use-this-plugin** from the `/` menu. It shows you how everything works and helps you pick a first step.

Two ways to start any feature: type `/` in the chat and pick a skill from the list, or just type what you want in your own words.

Completely new to Claude or plugins? Ask **"What is a plugin?"** and Study Buddy will explain everything from the beginning, step by step.

Not sure where to start? Say **"set up study buddy"** and answer a few questions about your goal, deadline, material and free time.

## What you can do

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Set up study buddy" | Builds a day-by-day study plan from your goal, deadline, material and available time. |
| "Quiz me on [topic]" | Quizzes you on your material, math included. Say "flashcards" for flashcard mode. |
| "Make a cheat sheet on [topic]" | Condenses a topic into formulas, rules, key facts and common traps, as a Markdown file. |
| "Log my day" | End-of-day check-in: what you studied, what you finished, what went wrong. Suggests plan changes, which you approve first. |
| "How did I do this week?" | A weekly summary of your hours, progress and recurring struggles. |

## What you need

Nothing to begin. You can type, paste or upload your notes.

Optional: connect **Notion** or **Google Drive** under Settings → Connectors, so Study Buddy can read your notes from there or save your plan there.

## Where your plan and progress live

You choose during setup:

- **This chat** (default). Keep using the same chat, or a Claude Project.
- **A Markdown file** you keep and upload when you start a new chat.
- **Notion** or **Google Drive**, if you've connected them.

## Your data

Study Buddy only reads the material you give it or point it to, and only saves to an app when you say so. It sends nothing anywhere else.
