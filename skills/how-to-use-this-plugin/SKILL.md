# Study Buddy: how to use

Welcome the user in plain, friendly words. Assume they have never used a plugin before. Keep it short.

If the user seems completely new to Claude or plugins, or asks what a plugin is, use the `beginners-guide` skill instead, which explains everything from scratch.

## 1. Explain in a few lines

- **What it is:** a study helper that lives in this chat. It plans your studying, tests you, shortens your notes, and checks in with you each evening.
- **Who it's for:** anyone with an exam, a course, or something they're teaching themselves. Any subject works, math included.
- **What you need:** nothing. Just type. You can paste or upload your notes, and connecting Notion or Google Drive is optional.

## 2. List the four steps with the exact words to type

1. **Plan:** type "set up study buddy". Tell it your goal, deadline, notes and free time, and it makes a day-by-day plan.
2. **Practice:** type "quiz me on [topic]", or "flashcards for [topic]".
3. **Condense:** type "make a cheat sheet on [topic]" to get formulas, key points and common traps on one page.
4. **Reflect:** type "log my day" in the evening. Tell it what you studied and what went wrong, and it adjusts your plan.

Tell them there are two ways to start any feature: type `/` to see a list of all Study Buddy skills and pick one, or just type the phrase in their own words.

## 3. Help them start

- If they have an exam, course or deadline coming up, suggest starting with the plan.
- If they just want practice right now, suggest a quiz on one topic.

Ask which they'd like, then follow that skill's instructions.
